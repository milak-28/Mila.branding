# Mila Kalezic — Personal Brand Guide

**Positioning:** A curious, ambitious, people-oriented emerging professional interested in consulting, behavioral economics, project management, and operations.

**Audience:** Future employers.

**Adjectives:** Passionate, eager to learn, confident communicator.

**Values:** Ambition, curiosity, joy.

**Selected tagline:** Curiosity Into Action.

**Alternate taglines:** People. Ideas. Impact. / Think Deeply. Act Boldly. / Driven by Curiosity.

## Three directions
1. **Modern Classic (selected):** MK serif monogram, restrained gold accent, dark green, balanced proportions.
2. **Curious Mind:** Organic circular forms and interconnected lines; people-centered and exploratory.
3. **Bold Strategist:** Geometric initials and stronger contrast; precise and structured.

## Design tokens
- Deep green: `#1B3A2E` — grounded, trustworthy

## Reflection (121 words)
I chose a refined MK monogram because it presents my name clearly while creating a recognizable symbol I can use across professional settings. The balanced letterforms communicate confidence and stability, while the curved gold line suggests curiosity, growth, and an openness to new ideas. Deep green conveys trust and thoughtfulness; warm gold adds optimism and energy. Together, these colors reflect my ambition, curiosity, and joy. The design is intentionally simple so it remains legible on a website, presentation, business card, or profile image. My tagline, “Curiosity Into Action,” connects my interest in behavioral economics and understanding people with my interests in consulting, project management, and operations, where strong ideas need thoughtful execution to make a meaningful difference.

## Quality check
Review at 32 px, in monochrome, and on light/dark backgrounds. Before professional use, check similarity to existing trademarks and customize monogram kerning.
